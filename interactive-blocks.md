# Airdrops

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-01 092537.png" alt="" width="248"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Airdrops are a key part of DopeRaider’s reward system, designed to give early supporters and active players a head start and ongoing advantages. From pre-launch incentives to major in-game events, airdrops provide valuable resources that fuel production, trade, and raiding.

#### Pre-Launch Airdrops

Players who participate in early testing or community activities may receive airdrops of essential in-game items such as:

* **Seeds** for weed cultivation
* **Chemicals** for cocaine production
* **DR coins** for unlocking restricted in-game features

These resources give early players the tools they need to produce, trade, and raid effectively when the full economy goes live.



Public & Event-Based Airdrops

Airdrops will continue throughout the life of the game, rewarding active participation and competitive success. Players can earn airdrops through:

* **In-Game Competitions**: Seasonal events, raiding contests, and production challenges with airdrop rewards for top performers
* **Partnership Promotions**: Special airdrops linked to external collaborations and promotional campaigns
* **Community Milestones**: Player count achievements, feature launches, or major economic events triggering global airdrop rewards



Why Airdrops Matter

Airdrops ensure players at all levels have the opportunity to earn resources, stay competitive, and build their empire. They drive player engagement, stimulate the economy, and reward those who actively contribute to the world of DopeRaider.
