# DopeRaider

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/photo_2025-06-26_22-13-29.jpg" alt="" width="128"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

DopeRaider  is an on-chain multiplayer RPG where players produce, trade, and raid to generate real income in a decentralized economy. With DINERO a fully backed USDC backed stable coin as the in-game currency, all profits translate to real-world value.

The game's economy is entirely player-driven. Your role, whether as a Producer, Trader, Raider, or a combination of all three, determines your success. The system rewards strategy, timing, and risk management, ensuring a skill-based, no pay-to-win environment
