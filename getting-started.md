# Getting Started



<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-06-27 093831.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Getting into DopeRaider is simple, whether you're a crypto native or new to Web3. We’ve designed multiple login options so you can start playing in just a few clicks  no complicated wallet setup required.

#### Login Options

**1. Social Login**&#x20;

* Log in with **Telegram**, **Google**, or **Discord**
* We automatically create a secure wallet for you
* Telegram users bring their **username and avatar** directly into the game
* In-app wallets allow easy sending and receiving of tokens  no technical setup required

Wallet & Network

* DopeRaider automatically creates a secure in-app wallet for you
* No separate wallet setup required to start playing
* Mainnet details will be shared closer to launch



Inventory & Stats

Your in-game **Inventory** displays:

* **Product Holdings** (weed, cocaine, etc.)
* **Consumables** (seeds, chemicals)
* **Upgrades** and their active durations
* **Respect** is your core progression stat
* **Trophies** earned achievements that show your status

Detailed inventory screens make it easy to track your assets, manage upgrades, and monitor your progression.



FAQs

**Do I need a wallet to play?**\
No. DopeRaider automatically creates a secure in-app wallet for you when you log in.

**How do I log in with Telegram?**\
Simply connect your Telegram account. We create a wallet for you and link your Telegram username and avatar to your profile.

Getting started with DopeRaider is fast, flexible, and built to suit both Web3 newcomers and experienced players. Whether you're testing with us now or preparing for full launch, you're only a few clicks away from joining the underworld.
